# 🚀 Arcov — the app I build and ship

**[Arcov](https://arcov.app)** is a private daily check-in app for long-distance couples — live on the App Store and Google Play under **Tuwar Corporation**. I designed, built, and shipped all of it: mobile, backend, native widgets, crypto and store presence.

[![App Store](https://img.shields.io/badge/App%20Store-0D96F6?style=for-the-badge&logo=app-store&logoColor=white)](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/arcov-couples-long-distance/id6761771701)
[![Google Play](https://img.shields.io/badge/Google%20Play-414141?style=for-the-badge&logo=google-play&logoColor=white)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tuwarcorp.arcov)
[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/arcov.app-1f2937?style=for-the-badge&logo=safari&logoColor=white)](https://arcov.app)

**Real end-to-end encryption, not a marketing line.** Content is sealed with
XChaCha20-Poly1305 AEAD; partner keys are X25519, derived through HKDF-SHA256
from a device-held master seed that never leaves the keychain. Account recovery
is a BIP39 mnemonic, plus a Shamir secret-sharing scheme that lets your partner
help you recover a lost device without either the server or the partner ever
holding your key.

**Native where it counts.** ~6k lines of Swift and Kotlin behind a WidgetKit
target and a custom Expo module I wrote for Android home-screen widgets, so the
check-in works from the home screen without opening the app.

**The backend is mine too.** 27 Supabase edge functions and 100+ SQL migrations
driving pairing, streaks, scheduled nudges and push, on top of ~70k lines of
TypeScript.
